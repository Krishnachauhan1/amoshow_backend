Delete your account

Last updated: 23 September 2026  ·  App: AMOSHWZ (com.amoshwz)

You can request permanent deletion of your AMOSHWZ account and the personal data linked to it. This page is the Google Play account-deletion path for the AMOSHWZ Android app.

How to request deletion

  1. Fill the form below or email privacy@amoshwz.com from the email used in the app.
  2. Use the subject line Delete AMOSHWZ account and include the name on your account.
  3. We will verify the request and delete the account within 14 days.

Account deletion request

Send deletion request

What we delete

  • Your account profile (name, email, password, profile photo)
  • Login tokens so you can no longer sign in
  • Watch history and continue-watching data
  • Likes, comments, subscriptions, and collaboration requests you created
  • Your channel, uploaded videos, shorts, and thumbnails (if you have a creator channel)

What we may keep

  • Payment and invoice records required for tax, fraud, or accounting (processed via Razorpay)
  • Data we must keep by law, for a limited period
  • Anonymized or aggregated analytics that no longer identify you

Uninstalling the app only removes files on that device. It does not delete your AMOSHWZ account. Use this page to delete the account.

Subscriptions

If you have an active paid plan, cancel it before or while requesting deletion. Refunds follow the payment provider and our purchase terms. Deleting the account ends access to Premium and purchased titles on AMOSHWZ.

After deletion

Deletion is permanent. You will need a new account if you want to use AMOSHWZ again. Public comments or collab history shown to other users may be removed or shown as a deleted user.
